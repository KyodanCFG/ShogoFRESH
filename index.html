The game

Thirty feet tall, or five foot ten

Shogo: Mobile Armor Division was Monolith's 1998 love letter to anime mecha: a fast, bloody FPS where one level puts you in a transforming mobile armor towering over a city, and the next sends you down its corridors on foot. It came out weeks before Half-Life and history looked the other way. It deserved better. This project's whole job is to make playing it in the 2020s effortless.

What FRESH adds

The 1998 game, minus the 1998 friction

Modern display

Widescreen with correct FOV, a HUD that scales with your monitor, borderless window, high resolutions. No config-file surgery.

A launcher that does the work

Server browser with one-click join, hosting, mod management, settings with explanations, keybinds you can actually change.

Servers that stay alive

A rebuilt dedicated server with bots that hold seats until humans arrive, hand them over, and come back after. Empty-server night is over.

Bots with temperament

Rushers, snipers, lurkers. They lose you in the dark, hear gunfire through walls and come looking, run for medkits when hurt, and you can teach them patrol routes by walking the route yourself.

New game modes

TOWs Out (every pickup is a rocket launcher) and Squishie (fight mechs on foot at one-fifth scale). Stock clients can join both.

Classic, when you want 1998

A Classic ruleset that restores the original balance to the pixel, and full compatibility both ways: FRESH clients on stock servers, stock clients on FRESH servers.

A real mapping kit

ShogoMAKE: build Shogo levels in TrenchBroom with tutorials, checkers, working example maps, and a one-command compile-and-launch loop.

Hundreds of fixes

Crash fixes, netcode repair, security holes closed, a kill feed that names the right weapon. Every one recorded in a changelog that says how it was found.

How this is built

ShogoFRESH is built by one person with heavy AI assistance, and every change is verified in play against the real game before it ships.

The game's art is untouched. Every texture, model, sound, level, and line of story is Monolith's original 1998 work. Nothing here is generated art. Think film restoration: the art is the artifact, and the machinery around it got rebuilt.

The receipts are public: a changelog covering every release, with how each change was found. If a project built this way isn't for you, that's a fair choice; the servers here welcome unmodified clients precisely so nobody has to pick a side. The longer statement is in the README.

Get started

Three steps

  • Own Shogo. Steam or GOG, a few dollars on sale.
  • Download ShogoFRESH from the releases page, unzip anywhere, run the launcher, point it at your install.
  • Play. Campaign, or the server browser. Want to build levels? ShogoMAKE is the kit.
